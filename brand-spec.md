## Design read

- Artifact: multi-page public institutional programme website.
- Audience: mentors, mentees, youth and early-career practitioners, institutions, employers, civil society, development partners, and prospective collaborators.
- Visual language: warm editorial institutional; structured, human, and quietly optimistic.
- Mode: greenfield.
- Visual variance: 6/10.
- Motion intensity: 3/10.
- Information density: 5/10.
- Asset dependence: 8/10.
- Brand fidelity: 10/10.

## Design decisions

- Design movement: editorial information architecture with warm humanist cues.
- Core principles: ABI-led clarity; intergenerational warmth; practical pathways; generous whitespace.
- Color philosophy: navy provides trust and institutional grounding; orange supplies human energy and forward motion; brown and taupe add warmth; paper keeps the canvas approachable and print-like.
- Layout paradigm: staggered editorial compositions rather than uniform centered cards; programme pathways and pull quotes create rhythm across the page.
- Signature elements: oversized numbered pillar markers; curved/rule motifs echoing the ABI logo; paper-to-navy field changes; a subtle moving hero frame.
- Interaction philosophy: navigation should feel like a clear invitation, not a maze. Every pillar ends with a specific next step.
- Animation: the supplied animation is the only hero motion; hover states use short ease-out transitions; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Typography: Montserrat Bold/SemiBold for headings and labels; Open Sans Regular/SemiBold for body copy and controls. Headings are compact and strongly tracked; body copy is readable and spacious.
- Brand essence: ABI connects experience, opportunity, and institutional capacity so people and organisations can develop sustainably. Personality: grounded, inclusive, purposeful.
- Brand voice: direct, hopeful, practical. Example lines: “Experience moves when people do.” / “Bring your question. Find the next step.”
- Wordmark & logo: use the supplied ABI mark in all branded contexts; do not redraw or replace it with a typographic substitute.
- Signature brand color: ABI Navy `#12405A`.
